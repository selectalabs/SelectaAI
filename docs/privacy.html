Selecta AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Selecta AI is a desktop app made by Laithy. This page explains what the app does with your data.

The short version

Selecta AI has no account and no server of its own. Your text, your chats and your keys stay on your computer. Nothing is sold, shared or used for ads.

What stays on your computer

  • Settings and AI keys. Saved only on your computer. Keys are encrypted with your operating system's encryption (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, or the Linux secret store when available).
  • All chats. The list of your ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini chats, and the text of chats you open in the app (if you turn this on), is saved only on your computer. You can delete it any time with "Clear all".
  • Sign-ins. When you sign in to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini inside the app, those sites keep their own sign-in on your computer, like a web browser does.

What is sent, and where

  • To the AI provider you choose. When you use a feature, the text you selected (and a screenshot, if you add one) is sent from your computer directly to the AI provider you picked (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, or a model on your own computer), using your own key. Their privacy policies apply to that request.
  • To Gumroad, for Selecta AI Pro. If you enter a Pro license key, it is checked with Gumroad (our payment provider) about every few days. Only the license key is sent.
  • To GitHub, for updates. The app checks GitHub for new versions. No personal data is sent.

Selecta AI does not collect analytics, usage tracking or crash reports.

Children

Selecta AI is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Website analytics

Our websites use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It uses no cookies, does not track you across sites, and does not identify you. The apps themselves send no analytics.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
