FAQ

Questions

Do I have to pay for the AI?

No. Google Gemini and Groq give free API keys, and the guided tour shows you where to get one. You can also use a model on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio, or a paid key from OpenAI or Anthropic.

What happens after the 14-day Pro trial?

Everything except Remember and Import stays free, forever. If you want those two back, get Pro for $7 a month or $49 a year.

Do you see my chats or my text?

No. There's no Selecta AI server. Your chat list is saved only on your computer, and your text goes straight to the AI provider you picked.

Can I use it at work?

Yes. Turn on Company-safe mode to allow only providers that don't train on your data, and follow your company's AI policy for sensitive text.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app where you can select text: Outlook, Gmail in your browser, Word, Slack, Teams, Notion, VS Code, IntelliJ and more. The default shortcut is Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Space, and you can change it.

Does it work offline?

With a local model in Ollama or LM Studio, yes: your text never leaves your computer. Other providers need an internet connection.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open your Gumroad library, or Settings → Selecta AI Pro → Manage or cancel. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

When is the Mac version coming?

We're testing the Mac and Linux versions now. Your Pro key will work on them too, on up to 3 computers in total.
